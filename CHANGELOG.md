# Changelog

## 0.4.0 — 2026-10-28

- Framework/direction suggestions precede editing. New imports require direction
  confirmation and explicit, independent caption/title choices before rendering.
- Four appearance modes: neither, captions only, title only, both.
- Confirmation stays inline in the workbench. Existing projects retain their
  prior appearance until changed; follow-up edits remain reversible.
- English-first repository presentation, Simplified Chinese docs, original
  bilingual artwork, license notices and manifest-checked source installation.
- Earlier 0.3.x capabilities retained: source-bound plans, local acoustic checks,
  persistent undo/redo with verified-render reuse, phrase-level subtitle fixes
  and optional cover candidates.

Complete word-level audio deletion, multi-source composition, picture-in-picture
and fresh-machine/marketplace certification remain outside this preview.
